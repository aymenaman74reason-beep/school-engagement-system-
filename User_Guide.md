# School Management System - User Manual

Welcome to the Integrated School Management System! This guide teaches you how to navigate the platform based on your assigned role.

## 1. Logging In
Access the base URL of the school system. Enter your school-provided **Email** and **Password**. The system will verify your role and authorize you automatically. 
- Ensure you have the `Remember Me` box ticked if you are using a personal, private device.
- The session lasts for a set duration; if it expires, you will be shown a notification instructing you to login again.

## 2. Admin Guide
- **Dashboard**: Review real-time school statistics including total active users, new enrollments, and active classes.
- **User Management**: Add new staff, block/unblock disruptive accounts, and re-assign roles safely without needing to touch a database manually. 
- **System Settings**: Audit logs automatically record any changes to the system for accountability.

## 3. Teacher Guide
- **Attendance**: Select your class and date. You will see a list of enrolled students. You can automatically mark "Present", "Absent", or "Late" in bulk.
- **Assignments**: Upload classwork and set due dates. You can also view digital submissions uploaded directly by students here.
- **Grades**: Update term grades on the fly directly per subject / student relation.

## 4. Student Guide
- **Dashboard**: Track your current overall subject scores and view upcoming assignments that are due soon.
- **Assignments**: See assignments generated by teachers for your specific class. Click "Submit Assignment", drop in your answers into the modal, and hit "Send".
- **Grades & Attendance**: Check your latest percentages on quizzes and daily attendance.

## 5. Parent Guide
- **Child Swapper**: If you have multiple children attending the school, use the Dropdown Navigation swapper at the top of the interface to rotate between profiles.
- **Child Progress**: Inspect individual grades.
- **Communications**: To ask a teacher questions, go to "Communications", click "Message Teacher", select their name from the list, and write a direct message!
